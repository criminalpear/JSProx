Using JSProx

  1. Browse. Enter a URL or search, then press Open.
  2. Run a script. Click the Console button on a loaded page.
  3. Fix a stalled page. Use Reconnect in the sidebar, or change engine in Settings.
  4. Open a cloaked tab. Choose Cloaking in the sidebar.

The arrow at the top hides or shows the sidebar. Your background and preferences save on this device.
